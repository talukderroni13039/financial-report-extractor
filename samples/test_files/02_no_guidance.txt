Harbor Foods Ltd. - Second Quarter 2026 Results Call

Tom Walsh, CEO: Good morning. Harbor Foods reported second quarter 2026 revenue of $612 million,
compared with $580 million in the prior-year quarter, helped by strong frozen-foods volumes.

Anna Reyes, CFO: Gross margin improved to 31 percent. As in previous quarters, we will not be
providing financial guidance for the remainder of the year.

Tom Walsh: Higher packaging costs and a tight labor market remain the key pressures on margins.
