Rheinwerk AG - Q3 2026 Earnings Conference Call

Klaus Becker, Chief Executive Officer: Rheinwerk generated revenue of EUR 2.3 billion in the third
quarter of 2026, an increase of 6 percent compared with the same quarter last year.

Sophie Wagner, Chief Financial Officer: For the fourth quarter we expect revenue of around
EUR 2.4 billion. Energy prices in Europe remain volatile and are a significant risk to our
production costs. Weaker demand from the automotive sector is a moderate concern.
