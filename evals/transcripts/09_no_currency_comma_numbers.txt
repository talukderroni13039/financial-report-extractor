Nordhavn Shipping - Second Quarter 2026 Results Call

Erik Lund, CEO: Good morning. Nordhavn reported total revenue of 4,215 million for the second
quarter of 2026, an increase of 3 percent on the same quarter last year.

Ingrid Berg, CFO: Freight rates were stable. We do not provide forward guidance. Fuel costs and
port congestion in Northern Europe are the main uncertainties for the rest of the year.
