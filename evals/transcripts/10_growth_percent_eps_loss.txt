Brightpath Biotech - First Quarter 2026 Earnings Call

Priya Shah, Chief Executive Officer: Brightpath generated total revenue of $640 million in the
first quarter of 2026, driven by higher sales of our lead therapy.

Marc Olsen, Chief Financial Officer: For full-year 2026 we expect revenue growth of 5 to 7
percent. Because of continued investment in clinical trials, we expect a net loss per share of
$0.10 to $0.05 for the full year. A delay in regulatory approval for our second product could
materially affect these targets.
